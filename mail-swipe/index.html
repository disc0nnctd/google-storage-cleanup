How Mail Swipe works

Each card is one sender with all their emails grouped together, biggest space users first. One swipe deals with every email from that sender in the chosen Gmail tab.

🗑

Swipe left ← Trash all

Moves every email from this sender to Gmail's Trash. Use for shops, newsletters, apps and spam you'll never read.

🕰

Swipe down ↓ Trash old

Trashes emails older than 1 year and keeps the recent ones. Good for services you still use (Swiggy, Amazon, Uber) whose old mail is just clutter.

✅

Swipe right → Keep

Leaves everything as is and won't show this sender again. Use for people, banks, tax and anything you might need.

Safe by design:
• Trash is Gmail's own Trash: recoverable for 30 days, then Gmail empties it.
• ↩ Undo (top) reverses your last action, again and again, even after closing the page. The Trashed tab can restore any sender later.
• Starred emails and emails you sent are never touched.
• Senders that look like bank / tax / insurance / ID / medical get a red warning and ask before trashing.

🤖 AI suggestions: every sender has been sorted by an AI into a category (Shopping, Banking, Social…) with a suggested action and a one-line reason, shown on the card. The suggested button gets a white ring. 🤖 Do suggestion (key A) applies it. Use the 🤖 menu (top) to show one category or one suggestion type, then Accept all applies every confident suggestion in that view at once (bank/tax-looking senders and unsure ones are left for you). One ↩ Undo reverses the whole batch. The AI only saw sender names, counts and subject lines, never email bodies.

Skip hides a sender for now (it's listed in the Kept & skipped tab).
Unsubscribe on the card opens the sender's own unsubscribe page, so new mail stops too.
Keyboard: ← trash all · ↓ trash old · → keep · S skip · Z undo · ? help
Gmail tab menu (top): start with Promotions, then Social and Updates.
